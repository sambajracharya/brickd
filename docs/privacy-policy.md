# Brick'd Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 8, 2026**

Brick'd ("the app," "we") helps you find and score foods that support
healthy testosterone, based on public nutrition data. This policy
explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and how to delete
it. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run the app,
we don't run ads, and we don't sell or share your data with data
brokers — ever.

## Data you give us

**Account (optional).** You can browse as a guest with no account. If
you create an account, we store your email address and a hashed
password (or your Google identity if you sign in with Google) with our
backend provider, Supabase. This exists solely so you can sign in.

**Saved foods.** Foods you heart are stored with your account so they
sync across your devices. As a guest, they stay on your device only.

**Preferences.** Settings such as dark/light mode and recent searches
are stored on your device, not on our servers.

## Data the app uses but does not keep

**Location.** When you open the Stores tab and grant permission, your
device's coordinates are used to query nearby grocery stores from
OpenStreetMap (Overpass API). Your location is used for that lookup
and is not stored on our servers or attached to your account.

**Receipt photos.** When you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to
OCR.space, a text-recognition service, to read the text. We do not
store your receipt photos or the recognized text on our servers. See
OCR.space's privacy policy for their handling.

**Barcodes and food searches.** Barcode lookups are sent to Open Food
Facts and food searches to USDA FoodData Central. These queries
contain the barcode or search term, not your identity.

## Service providers

Brick'd is built on the following processors, which receive only the
data described above:

- **Supabase** — accounts and saved foods (with row-level security:
  your data is readable only by you)
- **OCR.space** — receipt text recognition
- **OpenStreetMap / Overpass API** — nearby store lookup
- **USDA FoodData Central** and **Open Food Facts** — public nutrition
  data (no personal data sent)
- **Google** — only if you choose "Continue with Google" for sign-in

## What we don't do

- No advertising and no ad SDKs
- No selling, renting, or sharing personal data with data brokers
- No analytics that identify you
- No collection of health measurements — Brick'd shows public
  nutrition evidence; it never asks for or stores lab results,
  hormone levels, or medical history

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in **Profile → Delete
account**. This permanently removes your account and all saved foods
from our servers, immediately. Data stored only on your device (guest
favorites, preferences) is removed by deleting the app.

## Not medical advice

Brick'd summarizes public nutrition research. It is not medical
advice, and no food raises testosterone above normal levels in people
who are already nutrient-sufficient. If you have symptoms, talk to a
doctor.

## Children

Brick'd is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and
material changes will be noted in the app.
