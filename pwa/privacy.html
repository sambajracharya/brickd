Brick'd Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026

Brick'd collects the minimum needed to run: an email address if you choose to create an account, and the foods you save. No advertising, no data brokers, no identifying analytics — ever. You can use the whole app as a guest with no account at all.

Data you give us

Account (optional). If you create an account we store your email address and a hashed password — or your Google identity if you sign in with Google — with our backend provider, Supabase. This exists solely so you can sign in.

Saved foods. Foods you save are stored with your account so they sync across your devices. As a guest they stay on your device only.

Preferences. Settings such as light/dark mode, recent searches and your scan history are stored on your device, not on our servers.

Data the app uses but does not keep

Location. When you open the Stores tab and grant permission, your device's coordinates are used to look up nearby grocery stores from OpenStreetMap. Your location is not stored on our servers or attached to your account.

Receipt photos. When you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to OCR.space to read its text. We do not store the photo or the recognised text on our servers.

Barcodes and food searches. Barcode lookups go to Open Food Facts and food searches to USDA FoodData Central. These queries contain the barcode or search term, not your identity.

Service providers

  • Supabase — accounts and saved foods, protected by row-level security so your data is readable only by you
  • OCR.space — receipt text recognition
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass API — nearby store lookup
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts — public nutrition data (no personal data sent)
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google"

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad SDKs
  • No selling, renting or sharing personal data with data brokers
  • No analytics that identify you
  • No collection of health measurements — Brick'd never asks for or stores lab results, hormone levels, or medical history

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all saved foods from our servers immediately, and clears that account's data from your device. Data stored only on your device as a guest is removed by deleting the app.

Not medical advice

Brick'd summarises public nutrition research. It is not medical advice, and no food raises testosterone above normal levels in people who are already nutrient-sufficient. If you have symptoms, talk to a doctor.

Children

Brick'd is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and material changes will be noted in the app.
